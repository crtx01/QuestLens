## About

I'm c0rtex. We developed QuestLens to help make VR more accessible for people with
low vision, starting with one everyday obstacle: reading small text in games.

Quest has accessibility settings, but we could not find a built-in magnifier that
solved this in-game reading workflow on our Quest 3S. We wanted a practical tool
people could use now, while continuing to advocate for better official support.

QuestLens helps you read small text in Quest games: freeze the current image, open
a 2D magnifier, zoom up to 8×, then close the window and return to your game.
On the tested Quest 3S, a headset double-tap toggles the window and controller
triggers adjust zoom. Images stay in headset memory.

The app is experimental and designed with low-vision users in mind. It magnifies
a still image; it does not provide live in-game zoom or text-to-speech. The optional
gesture requires one-time developer setup and replaces the passthrough shortcut.

Please support the project if you can. Your contribution helps us maintain

Feedback on readability and usability is welcome, whether or not you contribute.
QuestLens is an independent project and is not affiliated with Meta.

## App integration

`app/src/main/res/values/support.xml` holds the public contact email and verified
support URLs. An empty support URL hides that provider. The home screen always
displays **CONTACT US** and displays **SUPPORT US** when at least one provider is
configured. Links open in an external app; capture has no support buttons and no
app feature checks payment status.

PayPal's link builder currently requires a business account for the signed-in
personal account. No PayPal support URL has been created or guessed. Resolve this
or choose an explicitly approved alternative before enabling its app button.
